Club Social Hub · mobile demo

A clickable demo of the Hub in CUNY 2025 colors. Everything runs on sample data. Nothing connects to real accounts, and nothing is posted anywhere.

Try this path:

  1. Tap Enter demo, then pick Club Lead. Or tap Walk through sign-up to see the first-time sign-up.
  2. Tap the yellow ＋ Create in the tab bar, then Post. Pick a photo and try the AI caption helper.
  3. Add alt text, preview it, schedule it, and see it on Home.
  4. Open Events for RSVPs and the event bar, and Clubs for forums, blog, tasks and members.

Then explore: tap your photo (top left) for You, drafts and Switch person. Try Contributor (Sofia Patel) to send a post for review, and Admin (Dr. Elena Vance) for the Student Life console, account requests and the audit log.

Refreshing resets the demo.
