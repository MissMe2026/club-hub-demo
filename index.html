Club Social Hub · mobile demo

A clickable simulation of the v2 mobile design, in CUNY 2025 colors. Everything runs on sample data. Nothing connects to real accounts, and nothing is posted anywhere.

Try this path:

  1. Tap Get started, then sign in as Aisha, the Club Lead.
  2. Tap Create post, pick a photo, try the AI caption helper.
  3. Add alt text, choose accounts, schedule it.
  4. See it appear on Overview, then open Upcoming events from the menu.

Then explore: every item in the menu works, including Calendar, Inbox, AI Assistant, Analytics, Forums, Tasks and Members. Sign out and enter as Dr. Elena Vance to see the Student Life console, approve account requests and watch your actions appear in the audit log.

Built from all 42 Figma v2 screens. Refreshing resets the demo.
